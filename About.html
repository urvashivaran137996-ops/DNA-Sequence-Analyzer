ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making DNA analysis easier to understand

A simple educational tool for exploring the structure and characteristics of DNA sequences.

OUR PURPOSE

Connecting biology with technology

DNA Sequence Analyzer is a college project designed to demonstrate how programming can be used to solve basic bioinformatics problems. It provides a clear and interactive way to examine DNA nucleotide sequences.

Users can enter a sequence and instantly view its length, base composition, GC content, AT content, reverse complement, motif matches, and possible open reading frames.

The project focuses on the fundamental building blocks of DNA: adenine, thymine, guanine, and cytosine. By presenting the results visually, the analyzer helps make sequence data more approachable for students and beginners.
